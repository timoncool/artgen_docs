# When something doesn't work

A troubleshooting page: on the left the symptom as you would describe it yourself,
on the right what is actually going on. Almost everything that looks broken isn't.

## I can't sign in

**The code doesn't arrive by email.** Most likely you pressed "send again" too
early: while the previous code is alive, no new email is sent, even though
the screen says it was. Open the first email. And check the spam folder.

**The "Get Code" button is grey and does nothing.** An anti-bot check runs under
the field; it is mandatory and computes by itself. Give it a couple of seconds.

**I press a social network button and nothing happens.** Social networks open
in a popup window, and a popup blocker or an aggressive ad blocker stops it.
Disable it for the site.

**The service won't accept my email.** On a first sign-in, temporary mail,
addresses with a plus and private relay addresses will not do. The check applies
to registration only — if the account already exists, signing in works as usual.

More about signing in — [Signing in and first steps](/en/docs/start).

## The search doesn't work

**Gallery search finds nothing or does not respond.** Search works only for
signed-in users. A guest sees the field but cannot use it.

**I search a long phrase and get nonsense.** Only the first ten words of a query
are used, the rest are dropped. Search with two or three precise words.

## Where did my filters go

**I picked colours and models, switched the sorting, and it all reset.** That is
how it works: changing the sorting clears the search, the chosen colours, models
and media type. Changing the category clears the search and models but keeps
the sorting.

The rule is simple: pick the sorting first, then filter.

**There is no palette button in the "Bookmarks" feed.** The colour filter exists
only in shared feeds and in your own "Created" — not in likes, bookmarks,
following or inside folders.

## The button won't press

**"Create" won't press.** There are several reasons and each is named in
the message: not enough coins, the simultaneous generation limit is reached,
the model requires an image or a mask, the prompt is too short or empty,
a required field marked with an asterisk is not filled.

**I uploaded a picture and the model seems not to see it.** Check whether
the "Custom Image" layer is switched on — a layer has a stack icon, and a dimmed
layer does not take part in the generation even though it stays in place.

**"Upscale with prompt" doesn't work.** That button takes the text from
the prompt field right now, and with an empty field it will not start.

**The heart won't click on my own work.** You cannot like or bookmark your own
work — there are counters instead of buttons.

**The report submit button is inactive.** The comment field and the button appear
only after a reason is chosen.

Full details — [Generator](/en/docs/generator).

## The model doesn't have the setting I need

**The setting was there, I changed the model and it vanished.** The set of
settings is decided by the model itself. If a block is missing, the model does
not support it, and that is normal: roughly every third model has no creativity
mode, every fourth no seed or negative prompt.

A hidden block is not cosmetics but a ban: the server refuses a generation if you
send a value for a setting the model does not have. And the other way round —
if the block is visible, it works.

**I can't switch a block off — there is no stack icon.** Then it is a plain
block, not a layer. Only layers can be switched off, and only on paid plans.

## I can't see all the models

**A friend has a model and I don't.** The catalog shows models with your plan in
mind. How many are available to you is visible in the "Tariff" filter in
the catalog — where you can also see how many would open on a paid plan.

The plan badge appears on a card only when the model is above your plan.
A missing badge is good news.

## Coins were charged and there is no result

**It looks like the money is gone.** That does not happen. Coins are not deducted
at launch but reserved: on failure the reservation returns to the same wallet it
came from. Ordered four pictures, got three — the fourth is refunded.

**My coins reset by themselves.** The daily balance is set anew each day rather
than accumulating. The non-expiring coins in brackets are untouched by this.

**The second number disappeared on my phone.** On a narrow screen the
non-expiring balance is hidden. The exception is the AI chat, where it stays
visible. The balance has not gone anywhere.

More — [Coins and limits](/en/docs/coins).

## I clicked the balance and the plans opened

That is not misplaced advertising: both balance numbers are one button, and it
opens the plans window — that is, topping up.

**I clicked the chest after claiming and got the plans again.** The chest changes
its purpose: open, it opens the claim window; closed with a timer, it opens
the plans.

## A work won't open or isn't visible

**Someone sent me a link and I get a message instead of a picture.** Three
reasons: the work is private, showing 18+ content is off for you, or the author
is blocked. None of them is about you or about the link.

**My own work is not in the shared feed.** A normal state: your own profile shows
more than the shared feed does. A new work appears immediately but takes its
place in the feed later.

**A work is blurred and I can't remove the blur.** Three conditions are needed at
once: be signed in, have a paid plan, and switch the display on in profile
settings. A guest has no such setting at all.

**A work tagged 18+ won't download.** Until showing such content is switched on
in settings, the settings open instead of a download. And on the free plan such
works cannot be downloaded in any format.

## I deleted a work — can I get it back

Yes, but not for long. Right after deletion a red plate with a restore arrow
stays in its place — until you leave the page, one click brings the work back.
The restore window itself is about an hour: after that the files are wiped from
storage.

If you simply don't want to show a work — don't delete it, make it private.

## How do I create a folder

You don't — and that is not an omission. Folders are created by the service, by
the day works were made. You cannot move a work to another day or give a folder
your own name.

Remember: deleting a folder deletes every work inside it, and there will be no
"you are deleting N works" confirmation.

More — [Your works](/en/docs/my-works).

## Promo codes and plans

**The promo code didn't extend my subscription.** If a paid subscription already
exists, a promo code does not extend it — it grants a temporary upgrade to its
own plan. On the free plan the same code is granted as an ordinary subscription.

**I moved to a more expensive plan — did I lose my paid days?** No. Moving up
recalculates the remainder of the old plan into days of the new one.

**I cancelled the subscription but nothing says I did.** The "subscription active
until …" line does not change after cancelling, because that date is the end
date. There will be no separate line.

**I paid and the plan is unchanged.** If you saw a "Payment in progress" window
after paying, that is a normal intermediate state. Wait and refresh the page;
do not pay again.

**The browser won't open the payment page.** Russian banks had their
international certificates revoked: install the state certificates or open
the payment in Yandex Browser.

More — [Plans and payment](/en/docs/billing).

## The chat behaves oddly

**There is no web search, reasoning or tools toggle.** Those are capabilities of
a particular model. No capability, no toggle. Change the model.

**I pressed "Retry" and nothing happened.** Retry works only for a failed or
cancelled answer. And any action is blocked while an answer is being generated
in that conversation.

**I edited an old question and lost the conversation.** "Edit and send again"
cuts off everything that came after that message.

**An attachment no longer works.** An attachment lives for one message. Attach
the file again.

**The answer is marked "Recovering the answer".** Not an error: the service is
pulling the answer back after a connection drop. You can wait.

More — [AI Chat](/en/docs/chat).

## The interface is in the wrong language

**There is no globe in the chat.** The chat has its own header, and on a wide
screen there is no language switcher in it. Go to any other section — or open
the side menu with the button at the top left: the language switcher is always at
the bottom of that menu.

**Part of the English interface is still in Russian.** That is true: the block
headings in the chat, the ready-role cards and some captions are not translated.
Go by the icons and by this guide.

## Service announcements

A coloured bar with an announcement from the service sometimes appears at the top
of the page. Close it with the cross and it will not show again — and it cannot
be brought back. So it is worth reading through before closing.

## How to use this guide

On the left is the list of sections, on the right the table of contents for
the current page. The table of contents is collapsed by default and has to be
opened with the button on the right edge — which is exactly why almost nobody
uses it. The left panel, conversely, can be collapsed to free up space.

The search shows up to ten results and states how many were found in total — if
what you need is not in the first ten, refine the query rather than scrolling.

<Callout type="tip">
  **Don't page through sections — type two words from your question into
  the search.** It searches the text of the pages, not just the headings, forgives
  typos and highlights matches. It also remembers your last query when you come
  back.
</Callout>

The search index is separate for each language: the English version searches
English pages.

## None of this helped

Write to support — the address is in the [FAQ](/en/docs/faq), along with a link
to the Telegram community. If the problem is with a specific work, include a link
to it: you can copy it with the button in the work window or straight from
the address bar.
