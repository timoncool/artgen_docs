# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I upload my own image for generation?

Use the "Your Image" function in the right panel of generation settings. You can upload an image from your device and use it as a basis for further generation. Please note that according to the service rules, you can only upload images that you have rights to. Do not use other people's images found on the Internet.

## What is the difference between PRO subscription and free access?

A paid plan opens a number of additional features. Exact figures are deliberately
not repeated here — they change. **The plans window always lists what each plan
includes**: open it by clicking your balance in the header.

- Save images in PNG and JPG formats in addition to standard WEBP
- Access to view and generate NSFW content (if enabled in settings)
- A larger daily coin allowance — the exact figure is written in the plans window
- Access to private mode, ability to hide your generations from other users
- More generations running at the same time

## I registered via VK/Yandex, how do I sign in by email?

There are no passwords in the service at all — sign-in is by a one-time code sent
to your email, or through a linked social network. Open **Settings → Quick login**
in the user menu and link an email or a second network there. See
[Signing in and first steps](/en/docs/start).

## How do I disable ads in the service?

To completely remove ads, subscribe to PRO. On the free plan, ads are shown to cover the costs of generation and image storage.

## I ran out of generations, where can I get more?

Every day each user gets a fresh daily balance — how much exactly depends on your plan and is written in the claim window itself. To get it, click the "Claim" button at the top of the screen. See [Coins and limits](/en/docs/coins).

## Why does one generation cost five coins and another two hundred?

Because these are different neural networks from different providers, and they
cost different amounts. The price is always written on the model card and next
to the create button — you see it **before** you launch, not after.

The counting differs too:

- **images** — per image;
- **video** — per second, so duration multiplies the price directly;
- **chat** — per thousand tokens, so a long conversation costs more than a short one;
- **voice-over** — per characters, not per seconds of sound.

<Callout type="tip">
  Sort the catalog by price, or just look at the figure on the card: a cheap model
  for drafts and an expensive one for the final piece is a perfectly normal
  working pair.
</Callout>

## Why can't I make a video on the free plan?

Because no video model is ever free: a minute of a video network's work costs
a provider far more than a picture, and there simply is no free tier for that.

You can check it yourself: open the [model catalog](/en/models/videos) and look
at the **Tariff** filter on the left. For video it will only list a paid tier.
The same check works for any other section — the filter always shows how many
models are available on which plan.

The free plan gives you image models tagged **Free** only — there are few of them,
and that is honestly visible in the same filter.

## Why is it so cheap? What's the catch?

There is no catch. The service is shared access to hundreds of other people's
neural networks: instead of taking out a separate subscription with every provider,
you pay once and use any model. Spending is counted in coins by actual usage,
so small tasks cost pennies.

## I just registered and I have zero coins

Daily coins are **not credited automatically** — you have to claim them with the
button in the header. While they are unclaimed, a green "Claim" is lit there.
See [Coins and limits](/en/docs/coins).

## My coins disappeared and I did nothing

Most likely it was not a disappearance but a reset. The daily balance is **set
anew** each day rather than added to the remainder: what you did not spend
yesterday does not carry over. Non-expiring coins — the second number in brackets —
are never touched by this.

## Why can't I reproduce someone else's work exactly?

Three things have to match: the same prompt, the same model and the same **Seed**
value. If the dice next to the field is green, the value is random — which is why
two runs of the same text give different pictures. Set the number by hand
to get repeatability.

## The model won't take my image

Not every model can work from a source image. How many images the selected one
accepts is written right above the upload frame ("Available to upload: …"),
along with the allowed formats and the size limit. Masks depend on the model too:
if unavailable, it says so under the button.

## Can I get coins back for a failed generation?

Nothing needs to be returned — it happens by itself. Coins are not deducted when
you press create, they are reserved; if the work fails, the reservation goes back
to the same wallet it came from.

## Why pay if there are free models?

There are only a handful of free models, and they are for images only. Everything
else — video, sound, the AI chat, private mode, PNG and JPG formats, an archive
of your works, several generations at once — starts with a paid plan.
**What exactly each plan includes is always listed in the plans window** — open it
by clicking your balance in the header.

## How do I change my display name in my profile?

Open **"Settings"** from the user menu, the "Profile" tab. The name is 3 to 50
characters, any characters, emoji and ASCII art.

The field is locked by default: **the pencil on the right only enables editing,
while the common button at the bottom saves**. Change the name, close the window
and nothing was saved.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Changing the name is available on paid plans only.** On a free plan the pencil
  is drawn with a gradient and opens the plans window instead of unlocking
  the field.
</Callout>

## How do I get my work on the ArtGeneration.me cover?

The path is this: first users **like** a work, and out of those that gathered
a response the cover is **chosen by the administration**. So likes are
an application, not an automatic pass: "got the most" does not mean "made it
to the cover".

What actually helps: make works people want to look at, and show them —
covers are published in the [Telegram community](https://t.me/ArtGenerationMe)
too. And like other people's good works: the feed lives on that.

<Callout type="tip">
  Don't confuse this with **"Rate:"** on the cover itself — that is a separate
  thumbs-up/thumbs-down vote on an already chosen picture, not a like on a work.
  More in [Gallery](/en/docs/gallery).
</Callout>

## What is NSFW content and how do I get access to it on ArtGeneration.me?

NSFW stands for "Not Safe For Work". This term refers to content that should not be opened in public places, around children, or at work, as it may contain elements of erotica, violence, or shocking scenes.

On ArtGeneration.me, the NSFW category includes artistic images with nudity and light erotica. According to our rules, NSFW images should not contain pornography, genitalia, or explicit sexual scenes.

Access is open to users over 18 **on any paid plan**. Three conditions are needed
at once — be signed in, have a paid plan, and switch the display on:

1. Take out any paid subscription.
2. Open profile settings, the "Profile" tab.
3. Tick "I am over 18…".
4. **Press "Save"** — without it the tick is not remembered.

<Callout type="warning">
  **The setting drops itself when the subscription ends.** After renewing you will
  have to switch the display back on — this is written nowhere, and the question
  comes up constantly.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  The same tick removes the blur in the gallery and unlocks **downloading** such
  works: while the display is off, the settings open instead of a download.
  More in [Your works](/en/docs/my-works).
</Callout>

## Is money automatically charged after the gifted subscription expires?

No, the gift subscription does not require payment and does not renew automatically. Payment can only be charged if you independently link a card and subscribe to a paid subscription.

## How do I make a Full HD or 4k image or other specific resolution?

Every model is trained on its own resolutions, so the set of available sizes is decided by the model — a trade-off between quality and speed. Resolution is picked in two steps: first a quality preset (1K, 2K, 3K), then proportions with a slider.

From there the size is grown with the tools above a finished work: **Upscale**, **Upscale with a prompt** and **Outpaint**. Combining them takes you well past the original size. Final trimming is easiest in the built-in [editor](/en/docs/editor).

## Can I use generated images for commercial purposes?

According to our user agreement, rights to images created using our service belong to users, unless copyrights, related rights, and other rights of third parties are violated. However, text prompts are not subject to copyright. If you want to keep prompts secret, work in private generation mode. When publishing images generated by us, we ask you to indicate a link to the image page on [ArtGeneration.me](https://artgeneration.me) if possible.

## Is private generation available on the free plan?

No. Private mode is available **on any paid plan**, and on the free plan every
work you create goes into the public feed.

Privacy comes in two kinds, and they get mixed up: **the toggle in the generator**
decides in advance, for new works, while **the toggle on a finished work**
("Visible to everyone" / "Visible only to you") hides a specific existing one.
The second also requires a paid plan. More in [Your works](/en/docs/my-works).

## How do I add text to a generated image?

This used to be a general weakness of neural networks, but there is now a simpler
route: **the catalog and the generator have a `RU TEXT` tag** marking models that
handle Russian lettering. Filter the catalog by it and half the problem
disappears on its own.

If the model you need has no such tag, the old trick still helps: write
the inscription in UPPER CASE, put it in "quotes", place it closer to
the beginning of the prompt, and say that it is `text`, `logo` or `caption`.

<Callout type="tip">
  The most reliable option is to **add the text over a finished picture in
  the [editor](/en/docs/editor)**: it opens straight on the "Annotation" tab with
  the "Text" tool selected, offering font, size, weight and colour. No model will
  ever spell a word as confidently as you can type it.
</Callout>

## I can't create what I want, what should I do?

The art of writing quality prompt requests, called "prompt engineering", is a skill that requires some practice. Usually users achieve excellent results after a couple of weeks of regular use of the service. Study examples of other users' works in the gallery, experiment with different formulations. If you need help, contact our Telegram community [https://t.me/ArtGenerationMe](https://t.me/ArtGenerationMe), they will definitely help you.

## How do I cancel my paid subscription?

Go to the "Payments" tab, which is located in the user menu. There you will see information about the current subscription and a button to disable auto-renewal. After canceling auto-payment, the subscription will be active until the end of the paid period.

## What payment methods are available for subscription and generations?

A bank card, **SBP**, **SberPay**, **T-Pay** and the **YooMoney** wallet. The list is shown in the payment window, where a method can also be saved for later.

<Callout type="warning">
  If the browser refuses to open the payment page, that is not the service. Russian banks had their international certificates revoked; the payment window itself offers to install the state certificates or to open the payment in Yandex Browser, which already has them built in.
</Callout>

## How do I download all my generated images?

In the "Settings" section, find the "Download my images" item, choose the period and start the build. The link arrives by email and as a notification on the site; its expiry date is shown next to the archive itself. The function is available on paid plans. Note that an archive holds **compressed** versions, not originals — see [Profile and settings](/en/docs/profile).

## Can I organize images into folders and albums?

Folders exist, but you don't create them — the service groups your works by generation date on its own. The folder button sits next to your works feed both in the generator and in your profile: open a folder to see everything made that day. A folder can be deleted (together with its works), but there is no way to make a folder of your own or move works between folders.

## What should I do if I notice generated images that violate the service rules?

If you see content that violates our rules (contains shock content, excessive violence, pornography, extremism propaganda, etc.), use the "Report" function. Moderators will review your complaint and take necessary action.

## Why is there no Google sign-in?

Google sign-in is disabled for Russian users to comply with local legislation.
The available methods are always listed on the sign-in screen itself, and signing
in **by email** — a one-time code, no password — always works.

<Callout type="tip">
  **But Google can be linked to an account that already exists.** Create
  the account with an email code, open "Settings → Quick login" and add Google —
  from then on you can sign in with one click. Signing in with it **for the first
  time** is impossible; linking it is not.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **The set of networks on the sign-in screen and in settings may differ.** If "Settings → Quick login" shows a different list from the sign-in screen, that is not an error: some methods are meant for linking to an existing account. See [Profile and settings](/en/docs/profile).
</Callout>
