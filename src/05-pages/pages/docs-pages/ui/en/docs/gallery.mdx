# Gallery

The gallery is the shared feed of works made by other people, and at the same
time the home page of the service. It is worth visiting before the generator:
ten minutes here show what neural networks are actually capable of, which models
made it and which words describe it. Any prompt you like can be taken with
a single click.

![The gallery home page](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-02-home-cover-feed.en.webp)

## How the screen is laid out

Top to bottom: the header, the **category ribbon** of chips, the **cover** with
a search field in the middle, the feed controls, and the feed itself.

## The cover and voting for it

Below the categories there is one large work. Next to it are **"copy prompt"** and **"open
fullscreen"** buttons, the author's name is a link to their profile (opens
in a new tab), and on the right — **"Rate:"** with a thumb up and a thumb down.

Your vote is remembered and the thumb becomes filled. A guest trying to vote
is asked to sign in.

A work reaches the cover like this: first people **like** it, and out of those
that gained attention the cover is **picked by the team**. Likes are
an application, not an automatic pass.

<Callout type="tip">
  **The cover picture depends on the section.** A category page shows a work from
  that category, and only if there is none does it fall back to a general one.
  So "why is the cover different every time" — it is supposed to be.
</Callout>

Covers are also published in the service's Telegram community —
[t.me/ArtGenerationMe](https://t.me/ArtGenerationMe). Worth subscribing if only
to see what level of work is at the top right now.

## Categories are assigned by the service

The category strip sits right under the header, above the cover: "All",
"Fantasy art", "Anime",
"Characters", "Landscape", "Cyberpunk", "Space", "Paintings", "Pop art",
"Cubism", "Architecture", "Interior design" and others. The strip scrolls
sideways.

<Callout type="warning">
  The category is assigned by the service automatically, the author has no say
  in it. The question "how do I put my work into the right category" has a short
  answer — you can't. And **"Other" is the default category**: that is where
  a work goes when no category could be determined. Not "the author skipped it",
  but the service's decision.
</Callout>

**Every category is a real link**, even though the feed changes without
a reload. You can copy it with the right mouse button and send it to someone —
and the address **includes the current sorting**, so the link passes on exactly
what you are looking at.

## Four sorting modes, and they really differ

![Sorting and grid density](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-08-sort-and-density.en.webp)

- **New** — strictly by time, newest first.
- **Rising** — works gaining attention faster than the rest. This is not
  "new with likes": what counts is the speed of gaining, adjusted by overall
  rating. This mode is the default on the home page — which is why the top
  is not the freshest.
- **Hot** — by the "gaining popularity" rating; ties broken by time.
- **Top** — by the work's overall rating; ties also broken by time. It differs
  from "Hot" not by period but by which rating is taken.

**Example.** Looking for fresh ideas on a specific theme — "New" plus a category.
Want to see the benchmark of quality — "Top". Want to catch what is taking off
right now — "Rising".

<Callout type="warning">
  **Changing the sorting clears the search, the chosen colours, the chosen models
  and the media type.** Changing the category also clears the search and
  the models, but **keeps the sorting**. The difference is subtle and causes
  a lot of confusion.

  Hence the rule: **pick the sorting first, then filter** — not the other way
  round.
</Callout>

## What is shown above the feed, and where

Half of the controls above the feed do not appear everywhere, and that is
the main source of "where is that button?" questions.

| Control | Where it is visible |
|---|---|
| Sorting | always |
| Feed search | always |
| Grid density | always |
| Scroll / pages | everywhere **except the home page and search results** |
| Media type: images / video / audio | **only on the home page and in your own works** |
| What to show from your works | **only in your own feeds** — not in a folder, not in someone else's profile |
| Bulk actions | **only in your own "Created" and folders** |

<Callout type="tip">
  The **"infinite feed ↔ pages"** switch exists only **in your profile's feeds** —
  "Created", "Likes", "Favourites", "Subscriptions" and folders. The shared
  gallery and its search have none, so the feed there is always infinite.
</Callout>

In page mode the pagination bar **sticks to the bottom of the screen** and does
not scroll away. And there sits **a number
field with an "of N" caption**. You can jump straight to page two hundred
without paging through. A wrong number quietly returns you to the current page,
with no error.

## Grid density and media types

The slider next to the sorting changes how many works fit in a row.

<Callout type="tip">
  **Default density is calculated from the screen width**, so on a laptop and
  on a large monitor the feed looks different with no settings involved. The value
  you choose is remembered and carried to other feeds.
</Callout>

The switches next to it filter **images, video or sound**.

As you scroll, a **"back to top"** button appears.

If nothing was found after changing the sorting or a filter, the feed shows
**"nothing found"** — and that state covers the starting grid. So an empty screen
instead of the previous pictures is not a fault but an honest answer to your
filter.

## Search and the colour filter

**Search in the service works only for signed-in users** — that is a general
rule, not only about the gallery. A guest sees the field, but both the search
and the detailed-settings button will open the sign-in window:
[how to sign in](/en/docs/start).

<Callout type="warning">
  **Only the first ten words of the query are used**, the rest are silently
  dropped. Search with two or three precise words: a long phrase gets trimmed
  anyway, and extra words only narrow the results.
</Callout>

**Search resets the sorting to "New"**, while the clear-search button returns
not to "New" but to "Rising" — the home page default. The behaviour differs,
and it throws people off.

**The query itself is checked too.** Type something forbidden into the search
and the feed comes back **empty — with no message at all**. It looks like
"there are no such works", while in fact the query was never run. So a suddenly
empty result on a seemingly harmless word is a reason to rephrase, not
a conclusion about what the gallery holds.

**There are two search fields, and they differ.** The one on the cover has
a refresh icon and a detailed-settings icon; the one above the feed has a palette
icon for searching by colour. They search the same way, but their button sets do
not match — a common reason for "I don't have that button".

The settings icon in the search field on the cover opens **detailed settings**:
two tabs — **Category** and **Models**. The panel shows the current selection
itself, as **"Selected category: …"** and **"Selected models: …"** lines — you do
not have to remember what you ticked. A good reason to open it when the feed
shows something other than expected.
The panel closes on a click outside it and after every search.

![Detailed search settings](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-06-search-settings.en.webp)

### Searching by colour

The palette icon **inside the search field above the feed** opens the colour
filter. That is exactly why it is rarely found — it is not a separate button.

![Colour filter](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-07-color-filter.en.webp)

The menu says **"Select colors (up to 4)"**. Clicking a swatch puts the colour
into the first free slot, clicking it again removes it. The selection stays
a draft until **"Apply"**; "Cancel" simply closes the menu.

<Callout type="tip">
  A few details that matter: **"Apply" starts the search straight away** — you
  don't need to press the arrow afterwards. **You can search by colours alone,
  with no text.** And a work must contain **all** the chosen colours, not any
  of them — which is why at four the feed usually empties. Start with one or two.
</Callout>

**There is no palette in the "Likes", "Bookmarks", "Following" feeds or inside
folders** — only in the shared feeds and in your own "Created". That is
the answer to "I don't have that button".

## Advertising in the feed

Ad cards appear between works. They are shown to **guests and on the free plan**;
**any paid plan removes them completely**.

<Callout type="tip">
  If an **"Ad space"** plate hangs there asking you to whitelist the site, that
  is not a broken tile and not a feed failure — it is your ad blocker. A blocker
  removes the ad but not the plate; only a paid plan removes it for good.
</Callout>

Ads appear in three places: the shared feed, the similar-works block and your own
works feed. The ads rotate as you scroll, so "the same one over and over" is not
a bug.

## The work window

Clicking a work opens **a window over the feed**, not a separate page.

![Viewing a work](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-13-image-page.en.webp)

At the top — the author, their plan badge and a follow button. Below —
**"Prompt"** and the text itself. Then the green **"Create your own version!"**,
which takes you to the generator with this work's settings already filled in.
At the bottom — the buttons and the line about linking.

<Callout type="tip">
  **"Create your own version" opens the generator at its own address** — so that
  link can be copied and sent on too: the recipient gets the generator with
  the same settings. Handy when you are explaining to someone "how this was made".
  A guest is asked to sign in first.
</Callout>

**You can flip through works with the ← and → arrow keys** — both in this
window and in fullscreen. The round buttons at the edges appear only when there
is somewhere to flip to.

**The page address changes when a work is opened** — so a link to a specific
work can simply be taken from the address bar, and the browser's "back" closes
the window instead of leaving the feed.

**The prompt is copied by clicking the "Prompt" heading, not the text.**
The heading is a button with a copy icon and turns green after the click.
People select the text with the mouse and then complain it was copied
truncated. The "Negative" block copies the same way — but it is only shown
if the work had one.

### The follow button and the plan badge

The follow button **has no words** — it is an icon: a "person with a plus" when
you are not following, and a tick when you are. Clicking toggles it. The question
"how do I unfollow" is answered by the same icon.

<Callout type="warning">
  **The author's plan badge is clickable and opens the plans window** — not their
  profile and not a filter. It looks like a label and behaves like a buy button.
  A free plan has no badge at all — just empty space.
</Callout>

### Your own work and someone else's look different

| | Someone else's work | Your own work |
|---|---|---|
| Left | **Like** and **Bookmark** — buttons | **counters** for likes and bookmarks, not clickable |
| | — | **Delete / Restore**, **Privacy** |
| Right | Copy link · Download · Report | the same |

<Callout type="tip">
  **You cannot like or bookmark your own work** — instead of buttons there are
  inactive counters. That is the direct answer to "why doesn't my heart click".
</Callout>

**A like and a bookmark are different things.** A like is public: everyone sees
it, and it works as the work's application for the cover. A bookmark is your
private shelf that nobody else sees. Both feeds live in your profile — see
[Your works](/en/docs/my-works).

On the free plan the privacy button on your own work is highlighted as a paid
feature and leads to the plans window: hiding a work works **on any paid plan**.

Under the work there is a line: **"When using the image, place an active link
to this page"** — with a link to the user agreement. This is the only place where
the terms for using other people's works are stated.

### The "Details" button: generation properties

The block is **collapsed by default** — which is exactly why most people don't
know what is in it.

![Work properties](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-16-work-properties.en.webp)

Inside: the **model**, **styles**, **creativity mode**, **resolution**,
**prompt adherence**, **seed**, **generation type**, **modification strength**,
**original image**, **creation date**, **LoRAs with their weights** and
a **colour palette**. Video adds duration, FPS, count and input media; audio adds
duration and format.

**The names here do not match the ones in the generator — that is a trap.**
Seed is called **"Noise"** in Russian, strength is **"Modification strength"**,
guidance scale is **"Prompt adherence"**, and the performance mode is
**"Creativity mode"** with Sketch / Draft / Masterpiece.

**A row disappears when there was no value**, and that is information too:
no seed row means the seed was random; no creativity mode means the model has
none at all, rather than "the author didn't pick one". If a model, style
or LoRA was removed from the catalog, the name is replaced with "Unavailable".

**"Original image" is a "Go to" link to the source work.** That shows what
an upscale, an outpaint or a variation was made from: you can unwind the chain
back to the very first picture.

Under the palette there is a **"Search similar by colours"** button.

<Callout type="tip">
  "Similar by colour" picks works by their range, not by subject: the feed will
  hold entirely different themes in the same colours.
</Callout>

## Similar works

The block under the work is **expanded by default**, and the button switches
to "Hide".

![Similar works](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-19-similar-works.en.webp)

<Callout type="tip">
  **"Similar" does not mean "similar in colour" or "by the same author".**
  The service matches works by **shared features of the content**: the more
  features in common, the higher in the selection. Works with an identical prompt
  are dropped on purpose — so the selection doesn't become the same frame ten
  times over. If a work has no features detected, the selection will be random.

  If you want "the same colour range", that is the other tool — the button under
  the palette in the properties.
</Callout>

## Video, audio and looking closer

![Video page](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-14-video-page.en.webp)

Video starts by itself and without sound, showing a cover frame until it loads.
Audio opens as a player with a waveform.

![Audio page](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-15-audio-page.en.webp)

A work can be opened **fullscreen**: the button panel in the top right corner —
download, zoom out, zoom in, expand to the whole monitor, close. The picture can
be dragged with the mouse, zoom is capped at three times, video zoom works with
the mouse wheel, and the ← → arrows flip works here too.

## Why someone else's work may not open

A link to a work can lead to a message instead of a picture. There are three
reasons, and none of them is about you:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| "You do not have access to private images." | the author closed the work for everyone |
| "You do not have access to 18+ content." | showing such content is switched off for you |
| "The image is unavailable because its creator was blocked for violating the site rules" | the author's account is blocked, their works are unavailable to everyone |

## Blurred 18+ works

To see such works unblurred you need **three conditions at once**: be signed in,
have a paid plan, and switch the display on in profile settings. A guest cannot
remove the blur — display settings exist only for signed-in users.

<Callout type="tip">
  There is a button over a blurred work that opens profile settings right from
  there — a shortcut instead of hunting through menus. On small tiles it is not
  drawn: with a dense grid there is nothing left to click.
</Callout>

The same blur covers not only "adult" but also **"disturbing"** content —
two different flags, one setting. More in [Profile and
settings](/en/docs/profile).

## Reporting a work

The exclamation-mark button at the bottom of the window opens a report form with
a list of reasons.

![Report form](/assets/docs-pages/screens/01-21-complaint-form.en.webp)

A report starts a review. What counts as a violation is described in the
[service rules](/en/docs/rules).

## Why your own work is not in the shared feed

The feed is built from two sources: the main stream and **freshly created works
that are pasted on top**. So a new work is visible immediately but takes its
"proper" place later.

<Callout type="tip">
  **"I see it on my page but can't find it in the shared feed" is a normal
  state.** Your own profile shows more than the shared feed does — including
  everything private.
</Callout>

**Video and audio live outside the main search stream.** That is why search
by description and the filters on the "Video" and "Audio" tabs behave
differently from images, and why their page count is approximate.
This is not a fault.
