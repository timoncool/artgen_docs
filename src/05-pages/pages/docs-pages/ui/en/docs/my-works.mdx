# Your works

Everything you create lives in your profile. It is also your public page: anyone
with the link sees your works there. It opens from the user menu, the
**"Personal"** item.

![Profile and the four feeds](/assets/docs-pages/screens/06-01-user-profile-feeds.en.webp)

At the top — your name, the settings gear, the plan badge with the subscription
date, and three counters: a heart, a bookmark and a person with a plus.

<Callout type="warning">
  **These three numbers are not your activity but your recognition.** That is how
  many likes, how many bookmarks and how many followers **you have received**.

  And here is the trap: below there are feeds with the same names — "Likes" and
  "Bookmarks" — but they are the opposite, they are what **you** marked on other
  people's works. Same icons, opposite meaning.
</Callout>

To understand which of your works land well, look not at the total in the header
but at the counters under the individual works.

## Four feeds, not one

Under the profile header there is a switch with four feeds, each with its own
counter:

- **Created** — your works. The counter adds up images, video and sound, so it is
  not zero even for someone who only made music;
- **Likes** — what you have liked;
- **Bookmarks** — what you have saved for yourself;
- **Following** — works by authors you follow.

<Callout type="tip">
  **A like and a bookmark are different things.** A like rates the author and puts
  the work forward for the site cover. Bookmarks are your personal shelf. Looking
  for "the one I wanted to repeat" — look in bookmarks.
</Callout>

Switching the feed clears the search, the chosen colours and models, and
the sorting goes back to "new".

### Your own profile and someone else's are built differently

| | Your profile | Someone else's |
|---|---|---|
| four feeds with counters | **yes** | **no** |
| folders button | yes | no |
| settings gear | yes | no |
| "subscription active until …" | yes | no |
| follow the author button | no | **yes** |
| report the author button | no | **yes** |

<Callout type="warning">
  If you followed a link to another profile and cannot find "your feeds" — you
  are simply not on your own page. There are counters in the header there too,
  but those are **their** likes and followers, not a switch for your feeds.
</Callout>

## Finding your work by colour

To the right of the search there is a palette button. It opens a window
**"Select colors (up to 4)"**: a grid of shades, a "Selected:" row with four
slots, and "Apply" and "Cancel" buttons.

![Colour filter in the profile](/assets/docs-pages/screens/06-01b-profile-color-filter.en.webp)

<Callout type="tip">
  This is for when you remember the picture rather than the prompt: "there was
  a red cloak against a dark background". Start with one or two colours — several
  combine as "and". The same trick works in the [gallery](/en/docs/gallery), only
  there across other people's works.
</Callout>

## Folders are dates

The folder button next to the density slider switches "Created" between a plain
feed and a folder view.

![Folders by date](/assets/docs-pages/screens/06-02-folders-by-date.en.webp)

Every work goes automatically into a folder named after **the day it was
created**. The card shows a cover from the latest work, the date and the number
of works inside.

<Callout type="warning">
  **You don't create folders.** You cannot put works into your own folder "Client
  project", rename it, or move a work from one day to another.
</Callout>

A few non-obvious details: **there are no empty folders** — a folder disappears
together with its last work, and that is not a fault. And **a restored work
goes back into the folder of its own day**, not today's; if that folder had
vanished, it is recreated.

**Deleting a folder deletes every work inside it.** This is not "remove
the grouping" but a bulk delete, and there will be no "you are deleting N
works" confirmation.

Looking for a work from last week — switch to folders and open that day.
Faster than scrolling a continuous feed.

## Selecting several works

The round button above the feed opens the bulk actions panel: select all, clear
selection, a counter of what is selected, a list of actions and a confirm button.

<Callout type="warning">
  **There is only one action in the list — delete.** No moving, no downloading
  in bulk: "take everything" is what the archive in [settings](/en/docs/profile)
  is for. And until an action is picked from the list, **the confirm button is
  inactive** — a common reason behind "nothing happens".
</Callout>

The panel opens by itself as soon as you tick the first work, and sticks to
the top of the screen as you scroll.

<Callout type="tip">
  The tick on a card is a small square, and a selected work gets a corner frame
  around it. **On a dense grid that marker gets lost** — if you cannot tell what
  is selected, lower the density.

  You can only tick works **in your own feeds and only in the feed**: there is
  no square inside an opened work. And **the selection lives until you reload
  the page** — it is a choice in the browser, not a property of the work.
</Callout>

"Select all" works on **the current feed**, so filter first and select after.
And note: a bulk delete is just as reversible as a single one, but you will
have to restore **one by one** — there is no bulk "restore" button.

## Delete and restore

It is one button that changes meaning: a **bin** on an ordinary work and
a **restore arrow** on a deleted one. There is no delete confirmation — you press
it, the work is marked deleted, and the button immediately becomes "Restore".

A deleted work stays in the feed in a special form — a red plate — until you
leave the page. That is exactly why one click brings it back.

<Callout type="warning">
  **The window for restoring is about an hour.** An hour after deletion a job
  starts that wipes the files from storage. Restoring within that hour cancels
  the wipe; after it there is nothing left to restore.

  This is the most practically important fact of the section: **changed your
  mind — restore now, not "tomorrow"**.
</Callout>

Deleting lowers the profile counters and restoring brings them back — separately
by type: deleting clips does not reduce the picture count. Deleted works also
drop out of the running for the site cover.

## Hide a work without deleting it

Every work of yours has a privacy toggle with self-explanatory labels:
**"Visible to everyone"** and **"Visible only to you"**.

A private work disappears from the shared feed, from search and from your public
profile, but stays with you. Switching back returns it to the feed.

<Callout type="tip">
  **Don't delete a work if you simply don't want to show it — make it private.**
  Deletion becomes irreversible after an hour, while privacy can be toggled both
  ways as many times as you like.
</Callout>

Privacy can be changed **by the owner only and on a paid plan only**. On
a deleted work it cannot be changed — you get a message saying the work is
already deleted.

This is not the same as private mode in the [generator](/en/docs/generator):
there you decide in advance, for new works; here you decide after the fact,
for one finished work.

## Downloading

**Images have a menu of three formats:** WebP, PNG, JPG.

- **on the free plan only WebP is available**; PNG and JPG are shown inactive,
  and clicking them opens the plans window instead of downloading;
- **SVG appears in the menu only for vector works** — and is available
  to everyone, including the free plan.

**Video and audio have no menu** — one button, the file downloads as it is.

<Callout type="warning">
  **A work tagged "adult" or "disturbing" cannot be downloaded until you switch
  their display on in profile settings** — instead of a download, the settings
  open. And on the free plan such works cannot be downloaded in any format.

  This is the most confusing situation of all: the button is there, you press it,
  and something else opens. That is why.
</Callout>

WebP weighs less and opens everywhere except old software. If the file is for
print or for a client — take PNG. And if you need everything at once rather
than work by work, profile settings have an archive build for a period — but it
holds **compressed** versions: [Profile and settings](/en/docs/profile).

## Reporting a work or an author

The form is the same for both cases. It shows the identifier of what you are
reporting, a warning that the report will be reviewed, **a choice of one reason**
and an "Additional" field.

There are eight reasons: pornography, exploitation of minors, fraud, violence,
discrimination, copyright, unaesthetic image, other.

<Callout type="tip">
  **The comment field appears only after a reason is chosen, and until then
  the submit button is inactive.** That is a common reason behind "the button
  doesn't click".
</Callout>

The reason **"unaesthetic image"** sits in the same list as the serious ones —
but it is about quality, not about breaking the law. Don't hesitate to use it,
and don't send it instead of a serious report.

What counts as a violation is described in the [rules](/en/docs/rules).
